Privacy Policy

VerifiedPickle · Effective: May 2026 · Last updated: May 14, 2026

VerifiedPickle ("we," "our," or "us") is a court-finder utility. We've designed this app to do one thing — help you find verified pickleball courts near you — and to collect as little personal information as possible to do that.

1. Information We Collect

Device Location (when-in-use only)

  • Foreground GPS — requested when you tap the location button on the map or list view. We use it in real time to show your position and rank nearby courts by distance.
  • We never request Always location permission. We never read your location in the background.
  • Your GPS coordinates are not stored beyond the current session and never leave your device.

Court Reports (optional, anonymous)

  • If you tap "Report a Problem" on a court (e.g. "not a public court," "courts closed"), we record the court ID, the report category you selected, and an optional free-text note.
  • Reports are anonymous — we do not link them to a user account or device identifier.
  • We use these reports to improve the public-vs-private classification of courts in the bundled dataset.

Favorites & Local Preferences

  • Courts you star as favorites, filter preferences, and any custom courts you add are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded.
  • This data is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Usage Diagnostics

  • Apple may share aggregated, anonymous crash reports and performance data with us via TestFlight / App Store Connect (only if you opt in to "Share with App Developers" in iOS Settings).
  • We do not embed any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

2. What We Do Not Collect

VerifiedPickle does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name or email address (no account / sign-in)
  • Your contacts or address book
  • Your photos, camera, or microphone
  • Your precise location history
  • Device identifiers used for advertising (IDFA)
  • Any data linked to you as an identifiable individual

3. How Your Data Is Used

  • Location (foreground only) — to display your position on the map and rank courts by proximity.
  • Court reports — to improve the accuracy of the bundled court dataset for all users.
  • Crash diagnostics — to fix bugs.

We do not sell data, share data with advertisers, or use data for any purpose not listed here.

5. Data Retention

  • On-device data (favorites, custom courts, filter preferences) — kept until you delete the app.
  • Anonymous court reports — retained indefinitely in aggregate form to inform classification. There is no user identifier attached, so individual reports cannot be deleted on request.
  • Crash diagnostics — retained by Apple per their data retention policies.

6. Your Rights

Delete your data

Because VerifiedPickle has no account system, "deleting your data" is as simple as uninstalling the app. This removes all on-device data (favorites, custom courts, settings) permanently.

Opt out of location

You can revoke location permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → VerifiedPickle. The app will continue to function — you just won't see your position on the map or distance-sorted results.

California residents (CCPA)

We do not sell personal information. We do not collect identifiable personal information to begin with.

European users (GDPR)

Our processing of court reports is anonymous and falls outside the scope of personally-identifiable data. Foreground location requests are processed locally on your device and never transmitted. If you have questions about your rights under GDPR, contact us below.

7. Children's Privacy

VerifiedPickle is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. The app does not have user accounts, social features, or messaging — all of which reduces children's-privacy exposure.

8. Security

The bundled court dataset ships with the app and never makes network requests for routine map browsing. The optional court-report submission and verdict-cache fetch happen over HTTPS to Supabase, which is SOC 2 compliant and protected by row-level security policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We will post any updates to this page and update the "Last updated" date at the top. We will not retroactively apply material changes to data already collected.
