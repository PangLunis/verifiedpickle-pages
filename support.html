VerifiedPickle · Help, FAQs, and how to report a problem

Quick Start

  1. Open the app. When prompted, allow location access — this lets us show your position on the map and rank courts by distance. (You can deny it; the app still works, you just won't see your dot.)
  2. Browse the map. Pickleball courts in your area appear as pins. Tap any pin to see details, satellite imagery, and any community reports.
  3. Filter by type — Outdoor, Indoor, Verified Public, Free, Reservable, etc. — using the chips above the map.
  4. See a problem? Tap a court → "Report a Problem" to flag bogus, closed, or private courts so we can fix the dataset.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why don't I see any courts near me?

VerifiedPickle ships with the OpenStreetMap-verified pickleball court dataset for the United States. If your area has no sport=pickleball tagged courts in OSM yet, none will appear. You can help by tagging courts on openstreetmap.org — they'll appear in a future app update.

A court is shown where one doesn't exist.

Tap the court pin, scroll down, and choose "Report a Problem" → "Not a public court" or the appropriate category. We aggregate these reports to clean up the bundled dataset.

The satellite preview is centered on the wrong spot.

VerifiedPickle uses on-device ML to re-center the satellite snapshot on the actual court surface once you tap a pin. If the pin is more than ~150 meters off the actual court, the ML may not find it — in that case the wider 275m view is shown. Reporting the pin location issue helps us improve.

Does VerifiedPickle work offline?

Yes for the bundled court dataset and your location. Map tiles and satellite imagery require an internet connection (provided by Apple MapKit).

Why is the app called "VerifiedPickle"?

Because every court shown has been cross-verified against an OpenStreetMap sport=pickleball polygon — a real surveyed court surface, not just an address someone typed into a website. Other apps list thousands of unverified entries; we filter those out so you don't drive to a backyard.

Is my data safe?

Yes — VerifiedPickle has no user accounts, no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics, and never reads your location in the background. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Does the app work on iPad?

Yes. Both portrait and landscape orientations are supported.

How can I add a court that's missing?

Tap the "+" button on the map (long-press where the court is located) to add a custom court locally. To get a court into the bundled dataset for all users, tag it on openstreetmap.org with sport=pickleball on a polygon, and it will be included in the next dataset refresh.

Reporting Issues

For bugs, feature requests, or accessibility concerns, email us with as much detail as you can:

  • iOS version and device model (Settings → General → About)
  • VerifiedPickle version (Settings → VerifiedPickle, or About screen inside the app)
  • What you were doing when the issue happened
  • Screenshot if relevant
